What to decide from this mockup

  1. Flow style. Bundled (the original "Flow" tree) or separate curves? Which one did your users have?
  2. Map look. Navy matches the dashboard, Midnight is neutral, Light is for light reports.
  3. Terminals. Bubbles sized by volume, plain dots, or nothing?
  4. Labels. Which terminal names should always be visible at dashboard size?
  5. Clicking. A click on a flow or terminal filters the side tables, and a click on a table row filters the map, the way Power BI cross-filtering works. Is that what users expect?
  6. Direction. Do the flowing dashes help, or are they noise?
  7. Maritime. Try it with the US and Middle East samples. Routes follow the sea lanes through the canals and straits Kpler recorded, and lanes thicken where routes share them. Does it read well, and should it be the default for long-haul views?

Everything inside the map frame is the visual itself. The navy cards around it stand in for your dashboard tables and for Power BI's Format pane. Drag to pan, scroll or double-click to zoom, hover for details, click to filter.
